# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate; focus criticism on ideas and code; respect privacy; and do not publish another person's private information.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and retaliation against good-faith reports. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or ban participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting mechanisms. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical and apply proportionate, consistent responses.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
